DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Crafted for those who measure life in moments, not minutes.

Every Lustro case begins as a single billet of 904L steel, brushed by hand until the grain runs unbroken from lug to lug. The dial is stamped, lacquered and stoned seventeen times before a hand ever touches it. Nothing is decorated. Everything is finished — a distinction our watchmakers consider absolute.

CALIBRE AG-72
THE
ASSEMBLY
212 components are assembled by a single watchmaker, in sequence, over three weeks. Every wheel is seated, every jewel oiled, every bridge torqued by hand — then the whole calibre is taken apart, inspected, and built once more.
001
SEQUENCE
The going train is built first, then the escapement, then the complications — never in parallel, never rushed.
002
PRECISION
Tolerances are held to ±0.02 mm. If a part does not seat perfectly, it is remade — not adjusted.
003
CONTROL
Assembled twice: once to learn the movement, once to keep it. 217 checks before the dial goes on.
